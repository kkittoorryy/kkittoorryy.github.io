Stream Clipper AI

A personal production tool for the VYBYZINK YouTube channel.

From a live stream to a Short

I use Stream Clipper AI to find moments in Twitch broadcasts, prepare vertical clips and add captions. The app keeps a record of the source and sends eligible clips to YouTube with the creator’s credits. Viewers can watch the finished Shorts on VYBYZINK.

How I use Google access

I connect my channel to the local app to publish Shorts, check their processing status and read their performance. A separate read-only connection to YouTube Analytics shows how long people watch and how many subscriptions the videos earn.

Only the channel operator uses the app. These pages explain its operation and are open to everyone.
